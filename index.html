The Montessori way

Children learn best when they can touch, choose and try again.

Dr. Maria Montessori designed her method over a hundred years ago. PlanetKids brought it to Libya first, and every classroom follows its core ideas.

A prepared room

Low shelves, child-sized furniture and real materials, all within reach. Children find things, use them and put them back by themselves.

Freedom to choose

Within clear limits, each child chooses their own work and stays with it as long as they need. Concentration grows naturally.

Learning by hand

Golden beads for counting, sandpaper letters for writing and sound cylinders for listening. Abstract ideas start as objects children can hold.

Guides, not lecturers

Our teachers observe each child closely, introduce new materials at the right moment and step back so children can discover the answer.

“Never help a child with a task at which he feels he can succeed.”
Dr. Maria Montessori
Programmes

Four stages, one journey.

Each class builds on the one before, from first independence in Nursery to reading, writing and maths readiness in KG2.

Ages 2 – 3

Nursery

A gentle first step away from home. Children settle in, build trust and start doing small things for themselves.

  • Practical life: pouring, washing hands, dressing
  • Songs, stories and first words
  • Short, flexible settling-in plan
Ages 3 – 4

Pre-KG

Hands get busier and minds get curious. Children sort, match and name the world around them.

  • Sensorial work: colours, sizes, sounds
  • Sandpaper letters in Arabic and English
  • Counting with real objects
Ages 4 – 5

KG1

Children start linking sounds to letters and quantities to numbers, and take on longer pieces of work.

  • Phonics and first words in two languages
  • Golden beads: units, tens and hundreds
  • Geography puzzles and science trays
Ages 5 – 6

KG2

The final year before primary school. Children read, write and work with big numbers, ready and confident for Grade 1.

  • Reading and writing short sentences
  • The thousand cube and four operations
  • Leading small projects and helping younger peers

The bead above each class is a real Montessori material: a unit, a ten bar, a hundred square and a thousand cube. Children hold each one as they learn place value, and they mark how far each stage takes them.

A day at PlanetKids

Calm mornings, busy hands, happy home time.

Every day follows the same rhythm, so children always know what comes next. The morning is built around the Montessori work cycle, a long, uninterrupted stretch for focused work.

8:00 – 16:00Sunday to Thursday, full day for every class
  1. 8:00

    Arrival and welcome

    Children greet their teacher, hang up their bag and choose a quiet activity to start the day.

  2. 8:30

    Morning work cycle

    Free choice of Montessori work across practical life, sensorial, language, maths and culture, with one-to-one lessons from the guide.

    3 hours, uninterrupted
  3. 11:30

    Outdoor play

    Running, climbing, gardening and fresh air in the school's play area.

  4. 12:15

    Lunch together

    Children set the table, serve themselves and clear up after the meal.

  5. 13:00

    Rest and quiet time

    Nap for younger children, books and calm activities for older ones.

  6. 14:00

    Afternoon activities

    Arabic and English language, art, music and movement.

  7. 15:30

    Circle time

    Stories, songs and talking about the day.

  8. 16:00

    Home time

    Parents collect children from the classroom door.
